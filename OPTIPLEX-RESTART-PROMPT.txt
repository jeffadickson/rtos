that count automatically. The immediate outstanding engineering task is modular
DMA. The portable layer now exists in include/rtos_dma.hpp and src/dma.cpp, with
tests/dma.cpp and examples/dma_polling.cpp. RTOS_DMA defaults OFF and requires

start/poll/stop/finish. Do not weaken these lifetime/ownership contracts.
